How it works

Four steps,
one question

  1. 01

    A market opens

    Firstprint opens a market on a new or trending token. Predictions stay open up to three days; the start price is locked when they close.

  2. 02

    You pick an outcome

    Choose where the price will land compared with the start price, and stake your free points. Earlier predictions earn a bigger share.

  3. 03

    The result is posted

    When the market ends, Firstprint posts the final price on the market page, so everyone can see which outcome won.

  4. 04

    Winners split the pool

    Everyone who picked the winning outcome shares the pool. Your points, wins and win rate show up on your dashboard.

Fair by design

New listings are wild,
the rules aren’t

Every market shows its rules before you predict, and they can’t be changed to anyone’s advantage afterwards.

Rules locked in

Once anyone has predicted, the start price, the ranges and the pool rules can’t change. The close time can only move later.

Early beats late

Earlier predictions count for up to 1.5× when the pool is split, so waiting to see which way it goes doesn’t pay.

Limits on every pool

Every person has a stake limit on each market, so no single player can take over a pool.

Refunds when it’s unfair

If nobody picked the winning outcome, everyone picked the same one, or the market is cancelled, every point goes back.

Open results

Each result shows the start price, the final price, the winning outcome and every winner’s payout.

A small, clear fee

Winners split the pool minus a fee shown on each market, usually 4%.

Questions

Good to know

How do I start?

Open the app and sign in with Google, an email code or a Solana wallet. Link a Solana wallet (or let Firstprint make one for you) and claim your 1,000 starting points as TestFPT, free. The app walks you through it. You can also claim free points every day: 50 on the first day, growing to 200 a day with a streak.

What is TestFPT?

TestFPT is your Firstprint points as a token on Solana’s test network: your 1,000 starting points, Earn rewards (tasks and invites) and daily streak points are minted to your wallet. With a Firstprint wallet, every prediction, win and refund moves as TestFPT too. It’s a test network, so TestFPT has no monetary value. Firstprint normally pays every network fee, so you don’t need test SOL.

Does it cost anything?

No. Firstprint is free. Points have no cash value and can’t be bought, sold or withdrawn.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Sign in with Google or email and Firstprint makes a wallet for you, with no seed phrase. You can also sign in with or link your own Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack): signing in only signs a message, and claims to it normally need no approval or test SOL.

How do tasks on X work?

Link your X username, open a task (follow, repost, share), do it on X, then press Verify. Each X account can be linked to one Firstprint account, and each task pays once.

How is the result decided?

The start price is locked when predictions close, so a token that climbs while predictions are open gives nobody an edge. When the market ends, usually 7 to 30 days later, Firstprint posts the final price from the exchanges or price sources shown on the market. The outcome whose range contains the move from the start price wins.

How much can I win?

Winners split the pool in proportion to their stakes, with a bonus for predicting early, minus a small fee. The app shows an estimated payout before you confirm.

Is Firstprint connected to any exchange?

No. Firstprint is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any exchange.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing here is financial advice. New listings are extremely volatile.
