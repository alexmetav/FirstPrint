# Firstprint video style (approved on video 1, X cut)

Use this for every new video unless asked otherwise. Reference engine: `x1.js` + `stage-sq.html`.

- Format: 1080×1080 square, 60 fps, ~22 s, H.264 crf 17 + AAC 192k, −14 LUFS.
- Type: lowercase kinetic lines, words revealed one by one (rise out of a soft lens blur, 0.34 s, outQuint), then the whole line grows. Never "Word. Word." captions.
- Constant motion: every scene slowly pushes in (2–5%); exits are scale + blur + fade.
- Object-led transitions: a UI element becomes the next scene (dot → pill, coin → card token, tap → green circle wipe → end card dot).
- Green screen wipe on the key tap is a signature move: keep it in every video.
- Real token logos (cryptocurrency-icons pack; official Solana mark from the user). The featured token flies out of the orbit into the card.
- Lists cascade in row by row; numbers count up; outcomes roll through one slot (slot-machine) instead of a long line.
- Motion blur on fast moves (reel steps, card entry).
- End card: small "Testnet live on Solana" pill above the logo, firstprint.fun button below.
- Sound: music bed + only relevant SFX (blips, tap, pop, soft land, shimmer, chime). No whooshes.
