# Firstprint launch videos (testnet)

Format for all five: 1080×1920 (9:16), 60 fps, about 15 s, H.264 MP4, no audio (add a track when posting).

## Style rules
- Product first: the app's own UI pieces (cards, outcome rows, countdown, chips), shot in tight close-ups with a slow, eased camera push. No stock shapes or AI-looking glow blobs.
- One idea per shot. A shot holds long enough to read, then the camera glides to the next detail.
- Type: Geist for headlines (semibold, tight tracking, two-tone white plus grey), Geist Mono for small uppercase labels and every number. Copy is written as normal sentences, never a string of one-word lines ending in full stops.
- Brand: near-black background, the five-bar logo (Moon green, Up green, Flat grey, Down orange, Crash red), white primary buttons, gold for time and rewards.
- Motion: every frame is calculated from time, so 60 fps is exact with no dropped frames. Ease-in-out moves, short settles, a soft press ripple where a finger taps.

## 1. The testnet is live
Purpose: announce, show what the app is in one look.
- 0–2 s: the five logo bars draw in one after another, the wordmark slides in.
- 2–5 s: "The Firstprint testnet is live." / grey line "Predict where crypto prices land, free."
- 5–11 s: a market card flies in; close-up on the token, the green Open pill, then the outcome rows Moon to Crash fill with crowd percentages.
- 11–13 s: a tap on Up; the row lights up.
- 13–15 s: end card: logo, firstprint.fun, mono label TESTNET LIVE ON SOLANA.

## 2. Make a call in three taps
Purpose: show exactly how to predict.
- Close-up of the outcome list. Caption: "Pick where the price lands."
- Tap Up. The prediction panel rises; caption "Choose your points."
- Chips 25, 50, 100, 250; tap 100. The estimate rolls up ("If Up wins, about 770 pts").
- Tap "Predict Up for 100 pts"; the button turns into a check, a "You 100" tag lands on the Up row, the pool counter ticks up. Caption: "Your call is in."
- End card with the URL.

## 3. The clock decides
Purpose: explain close, start price and result.
- Extreme close-up of the big green countdown running out. Caption: "Predictions close, and the start price locks."
- A lock clicks shut over "$0.0001822".
- The countdown turns gold and races down. Caption: "Then the clock runs to the result."
- Result: final price, +62%, Moon row marked Winner, 15.4×. Coins fly into the balance, which counts up. Caption: "Call it right and you share the pool."

## 4. Free points every day
Purpose: daily streak and tasks.
- Close-up of the streak circles filling one by one: +50, +75 … +200, flame count rising. Caption: "Claim free points every day."
- Grey line: "Keep the streak and it grows to 200 a day."
- Task card "Follow @firstprintapp on X  +500". Tap Verify, spinner with a seconds counter, then a check.
- Coins fly straight into the points chip, balance counts 1,000 → 1,500. Caption: "Finish a task for 500 more."

## 5. Get in early
Purpose: new markets, early bonus, leaderboard.
- A new-market post slides in (token name and ticker, start price, predictions close, result). Caption: "New markets open all the time."
- Featured card; the Early bonus row counts to 1.50×. Caption: "Early picks count for more when the pool is split."
- Leaderboard rows; your row climbs to #1. Caption: "Climb the leaderboard."
- End card: logo, firstprint.fun, TESTNET LIVE ON SOLANA.

All numbers shown are example values for the animation (no real traction figures).
