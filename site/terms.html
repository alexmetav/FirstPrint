Last updated 8 October 2026

These terms are the rules for using Firstprint. By signing in or playing, you agree to them. They’re written to be read, so please do.

What Firstprint is

Firstprint is a free prediction game about crypto token prices. You predict where a token’s price will land and play with points. Firstprint is not a betting, trading or investment service: you can’t pay money to play, and you can’t win money.

Who can play

  • You must be at least 18 and allowed to use a service like this where you live.
  • One account per person. Signing in with Google, an email code or a wallet that belongs to the same person counts as one person.
  • Keep your sign-in methods and wallet safe. We will never ask for your password, seed phrase or private key.

Points and TestFPT have no value

  • Points are for play. They have no cash value, can’t be bought, and can’t be sold or exchanged for money or anything of value.
  • TestFPT is your points as a token on Solana’s test network. Test networks use free test SOL and are meant for testing. TestFPT is not an investment and is not expected to have any value.
  • Solana’s test network can be reset by its operators. If that happens, TestFPT in your wallet may disappear. Your Firstprint balance is kept separately, and we’ll offer a way to claim again where we can.
  • We may correct balances that are wrong because of a bug, an error in a market, or a breach of these terms.
  • We may reset, change or end points, TestFPT, rewards or the leaderboard, for example when the testnet phase ends. Nothing on Firstprint promises any future token, airdrop or reward.

Markets and results

  • Each market shows its start price (or that the start price is the token’s opening price, or its price when predictions close), the exchanges it refers to, when predictions close and when the result is expected.
  • Firstprint posts the result using public prices from the exchanges or price sources shown on the market. Exchanges can show slightly different prices, and the result we post is final.
  • We may cancel a market and return everyone’s points, for example if a listing is delayed or cancelled, the price data is unreliable, or we made a mistake setting the market up.
  • Winners split the pool as described on the market page. Results and payouts can’t be appealed for value, but if you think we made an error, email us and we’ll look at it.

Early bonus and taking predictions back

  • Earlier predictions get a bigger share when the winners split the pool. Each market shows its bonus, which falls steadily until predictions close. The bonus never adds points to the pool.
  • You can take a prediction back before predictions close, up to the time shown on the market. Your stake comes back minus a fee shown before you confirm. The fee is small in the first half of the prediction window and rises toward the close.
  • Part of the fee is burned and part goes to players who predicted in the first half of the window and stayed in. Each market page lists the exact numbers under “Early bonus, taking a pick back and fees”.
  • Fees are not returned, including if the market is cancelled later. Taking a prediction back also gives up its early bonus.

Play fair

Don’t use more than one account, bots or scripts, fake or self-referrals, faked task completions, or bugs to gain points. If we see this, we may remove points, remove you from the leaderboard, or close the accounts involved. If you find a bug, please tell us instead: team@firstprint.fun.

Tasks, invites and rewards

Tasks on X, invite rewards and daily points have limits shown in the app. We may change, pause or end them at any time. Linking your X username only lets us credit tasks to you. We don’t post on your behalf.
