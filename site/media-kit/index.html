About Firstprint

Copy a description for your post, article or listing.

One line

Firstprint: call where crypto prices land.

Short

Firstprint is a free prediction game for crypto prices. Pick where a token lands (Crash, Down, Flat, Up or Moon) with free points and climb the leaderboard.

Long

Firstprint runs free prediction markets on crypto tokens: new exchange listings, trending tokens and well-known ones. Players sign in with Google, email or a Solana wallet and stake free points on five outcomes, from Crash to Moon, or on a Yes/No question. Good calls climb the leaderboard, and rewards can be claimed as TestFPT on Solana. Points have no cash value.

Using the logo

  • Leave space around the logo, at least the height of one bar.
  • Use the badge or icon on photos and busy backgrounds.
  • Write the name as Firstprint: one word, capital F.
  • Link to firstprint.fun when you can.
  • Don’t recolour, reorder or stretch the bars. The colour order is the meaning.
  • Don’t rotate the logo or add effects like shadows or outlines.
  • Don’t shrink the mark below about 24 px tall.
  • Don’t suggest Firstprint endorses something without asking us.
