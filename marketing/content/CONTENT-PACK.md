## 5. Blurb (for investors, partners, exchanges and listings)

### One line
Firstprint is a free prediction game where people call where a crypto token's price lands. Every result is public, so a good call becomes a track record.

### Short (about 60 words)
Firstprint runs prediction markets on crypto tokens: new exchange listings, trending tokens and majors. Players stake free points on one of five outcomes, from Crash to Moon, or answer a Yes/No question. Correct calls share the pool, with a bonus for calling it early. The testnet is live on Solana, where points move as the TestFPT test token.

### Full (for a deck, an application or a partner email)
**What it is.** Firstprint turns "where does this token go next?" into a clean, time-boxed question with a public answer.
- Each market has a start price and a result time.
- Players pick one of five outcomes: Moon (+50% or more), Up, Flat, Down or Crash (−50% or worse). Some markets ask a single Yes/No question instead.
- Correct predictions share the pool, and earlier picks count for more (up to 1.5× weight).
- Every result and every player's record is public, on a leaderboard and on profile pages.

**What's live today.**
- Markets on new listings, trending tokens and established tokens, with prices referenced from major exchanges.
- Sign-in with Google, email or a Solana wallet. Players without a wallet get a Firstprint wallet automatically.
- Daily streak rewards, X and Telegram tasks, referrals, results by email and a shareable PnL card.
- Telegram alerts for new markets.
- An on-chain layer on Solana testnet: rewards move as the TestFPT token and are sent straight to the player's wallet, with Firstprint paying the network fee.
- A free public read-only API for markets and the leaderboard.

**Why it matters.** Calls on X and in Telegram groups are never scored, trading a fresh listing means the worst liquidity of a token's life, and general prediction markets don't cover single-token moves over days. Firstprint fills that gap for free, and builds a dataset of crowd forecasts on token prices along the way.

**Business model** (from the whitepaper):
- *Now:* featured markets and launch promotion for projects and exchanges; exchange referral share; a market-sentiment data API.
- *Next:* Pro membership and sponsored tournaments.
- *Later:* a fee on value-backed pools, only where licensed and permitted.

**Token.** A proposed FPT token is described as a draft on the tokenomics page. No token has been issued and none is for sale. Points and TestFPT have no cash value.
