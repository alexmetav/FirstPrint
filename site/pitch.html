The problem

Everyone has a price call with nowhere to play it

01 · NOISE

Calls live in group chats

“This one will 10×” is posted in thousands of Telegram and X threads every week. Nobody keeps score, so the loudest voice wins, not the best one.

02 · RISK

Trading a view is brutal

Leverage, fees and sudden wicks, and for new listings, thin order books and huge spreads. Having a view on a token today means risking real capital.

03 · NO VENUE

General markets skip it

Prediction markets cover elections, sports and macro. Short-lived questions about where one token’s price lands, across many exchanges, aren’t served.

The solution

Markets on crypto tokens, five outcomes, one leaderboard

New listings, trending tokens and established tokens get markets that settle on real exchange prices; listing markets can open before or at listing. Players pick where the price lands against its start price. Correct calls split the pool; early calls earn a bonus.

How it works

Thirty seconds to play

Sign in, get 1,000 points

Google, a one-time email code or a Solana wallet. Email players get a Firstprint wallet made for them, with no seed phrase.

Pick an outcome

Stake points on Crash, Down, Flat, Up or Moon, or answer a Yes/No question. Odds move live as the crowd picks.

Climb the board

Results are posted from exchange prices. Winners split the pool, share a PnL card on X, and keep a daily streak going.

Why now

Three curves are crossing

PREDICTION MARKETS

Gone mainstream

Polymarket and Kalshi turned “trade on what you believe” into a mass-market habit. Users now expect a market for every question.

CRYPTO

A constant stream

Crypto trades around the clock, trending tokens change daily and exchanges list new tokens every week. Every price move is a fresh, time-boxed question: constant market inventory.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Cheap, fast settlement

Solana makes per-prediction on-chain records cost a fraction of a cent, and embedded wallets remove the onboarding wall.

Business model

Revenue that starts before real money

NOWFeatured markets

Projects and exchanges pay for featured market slots and launch-day promotion to an audience that is already watching token prices.

NOWExchange referrals

Every market links to where the token trades. Exchange affiliate programmes pay a share of fees from the traders we send.

NOWMarket sentiment data

Crowd forecasts on token prices, including new tokens before they trade, as an API for funds, market makers and research desks.

NEXTPro membership

Earlier market and listing alerts, deeper stats, private leagues and profile perks.

NEXTSponsored tournaments

Partners fund prize pools; Firstprint runs and promotes the competition for a fee.

LATER4% market fee

On value-backed markets where permitted, a 4% fee on each pool, shared with market creators once anyone can create markets.
