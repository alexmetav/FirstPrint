FPT

The proposed token for Firstprint: rewards for good calls, a bond for market creators, and a share of the protocol’s fees.

⚠︎

Draft proposal. FPT has not been issued and is not for sale. Every number on this page may change before any launch. Today Firstprint is a free points game, and TestFPT is a test token with no value.

The supply is fixed at one billion FPT. There is no inflation and no mint after launch: rewards come from the community allocation, and buy-backs from the fee reduce circulating supply over time. Under 10% of the supply is unlocked at launch; team, investor and advisor tokens are locked for a year.

Utility

PLAY

Rewards for good calls

Streaks, tournaments and leaderboard seasons pay out in FPT from the community allocation, weighted toward accurate players rather than volume.

CREATE

Market-creation bond

Anyone creating a market locks FPT as a bond. It is returned when the market settles cleanly and can be cut if the market is cancelled through the creator’s fault.

STAKE

Share of the fee

Staked FPT receives the tokens bought back with part of the market fee, plus Pro features and higher reward multipliers.

GOVERN

A say in the rules

Holders vote on listing policy, outcome thresholds, the fee split and treasury grants.

Fee flows

On value-backed markets, where permitted, each pool pays a 4% fee. It is split three ways:

1%to the market’s creator
1%buys back FPT for stakers
2%to the treasury for operations

Paying fees in FPT earns a discount. Off-chain revenue (featured listings, data and Pro memberships) goes to the treasury, and part of it can fund further buy-backs by governance vote.

TestFPT and the airdrop

TestFPT, which players earn today on Solana’s test network, is not FPT and has no value. Activity on Firstprint (predictions, accuracy, streaks and referrals) may be considered for the 5% testnet airdrop if FPT launches. There is no guarantee of an airdrop, and accounts found farming with fake or duplicate accounts are excluded.

Governance

At launch the core team runs the protocol, with the treasury in a multi-signature wallet. Control of listing policy, fee parameters and treasury grants moves to FPT holders in stages as the protocol matures.
