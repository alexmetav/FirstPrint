Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Firstprint is a free prediction game about new crypto exchange listings, played with points that have no cash value. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to have it removed. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your email address and name. We use the name only to suggest a username. We never see your Google password.
  • When you sign in with an email code: your email address, so we can send you the code and recognise your account.
  • When you sign in with a Solana wallet: your wallet’s public address. Signing in only signs a message. It never sends a transaction or touches your funds.
  • Your activity: your username, points balance, predictions and point history, so the game works and the leaderboard is accurate. Your username and predictions are shown publicly on market pages, the leaderboard and your public profile page, which also shows your win rate and points won. Your email address, wallet and X username are never shown there.
  • Your X username, if you link it: so tasks on X can be credited to you. We don’t log in to X or read anything from your X account. One X username can be linked to one Firstprint account.
  • Invites: if you sign up through a friend’s invite link, we record who invited you so they can get their reward.
  • TestFPT claims: when you claim, we record the wallet you claimed to and the transaction. Transactions on Solana’s public test network, including your wallet address and the TestFPT you receive, are visible to anyone on that network.
  • A sign-in cookie: one cookie (fp_session) that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • Your IP address: used briefly to stop abuse, such as too many sign-in attempts. It is kept in memory and not stored with your account.

The practice version

The practice version at /play/ and the practice market on the home page keep everything in your own browser’s storage. Nothing from them is sent to us.

Who helps us run Firstprint

These companies process data for us only to provide their service:

  • Render hosts the app and its database.
  • Supabase stores private backup copies of the database.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google provides “Continue with Google” sign-in.
  • Resend sends sign-in code emails.

Exchange market data comes from CoinGecko and the exchanges themselves. No personal data is sent to them.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and activity while your account exists. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted. We also keep private daily backup copies of the database. If you delete your account, older backups may still contain your data until those copies are deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your account and its data at any time by emailing team@firstprint.fun from the address you signed in with, or by telling us your wallet address. We’ll reply within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your email, wallet link and points.

Age

Firstprint is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For anything that significantly affects how we use your data, we’ll also tell you in the app.

Contact
