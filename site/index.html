How it works

Four steps, one question,
settled on real prices.

  1. 01

    A listing appears

    Firstprint watches exchange announcements and new trading pairs, then opens a market the moment a listing is confirmed.

  2. 02

    You pick an outcome

    Choose where the price lands 72 hours after trading opens. Predict early and your share grows.

  3. 03

    Prices are measured

    The start is the average of the first hour. The finish is the average of the last hour. No single wick decides it.

  4. 04

    Winners split the pool

    Everyone who called it right shares the pool, and results publish the exact prices used.

Fair by design

New listings are wild.
The rules aren’t.

Six rules keep a single trade, whale, or insider from deciding any market.

Averages, not spikes

Start and finish prices are one-hour averages, so a wick can’t move the result.

More than one exchange

When a token trades in several places, a volume-weighted median is used.

Limits on every pool

Every pool has a size limit and every person has a stake limit.

Early beats late

Earlier predictions count for more, so waiting to read the chart doesn’t pay.

Automatic refunds

Delays, halted trading, thin data, or no winners mean everyone gets their stake back.

Open results

Every settlement publishes the prices used and a fingerprint anyone can check.

Questions

Good to know.

When can I start predicting?

Predictions are coming soon. The exchange explorer is live today and the listing radar is next. Follow our updates to hear the moment predictions open.

Does it cost anything?

No. Predictions will use free points that have no cash value.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

You’ll sign in with a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack. Signing in only signs a message. It never sends a transaction or costs fees.

Which exchanges are covered?

The explorer shows Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Upbit, Bitget, Gate, KuCoin, MEXC, Kraken, HTX, Crypto.com, and Bitfinex. Listing tracking starts with Binance, MEXC, Bybit, OKX, Gate, Bitget, and KuCoin.

Where does the data come from?

Exchange volume, trust scores, and pairs come from CoinGecko. Listing tracking and settlement prices come from each exchange’s own public API.

Is Firstprint connected to any exchange?

No. Firstprint is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any exchange.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing here is financial advice. New listings are extremely volatile, and some features may not be available in every country.
